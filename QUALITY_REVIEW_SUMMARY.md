# Quality Review & Correction Log

**Project:** Aarong: Brand Audit & Competitor Intelligence  
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Review type:** Independent factual, methodological and cross-file consistency review of the supplied portfolio archive.

## Corrections applied

### 1. Guinness World Records category
The report now uses the official title **"Largest craft and artisanal retail store"** for the 2,636 m² Dhanmondi flagship, achieved on 30 October 2025. The Nakshi Kantha installation is described separately as an in-store feature, not as the record category.

Source: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/777028-largest-craft-and-artisanal-retail-store

### 2. BRAC surplus model
The report now explains BRAC's published enterprise-wide model: 50% of surplus is reinvested across BRAC Enterprises and 50% supports BRAC's core fund and wider expenditures. It explicitly says that the cited publication does not disclose Aarong-specific audited surplus allocation and does not evidence artisan dividends.

Source: https://www.brac.net/sites/default/files/Overview-BRAC-enterprises-and-investments.pdf

### 3. Retail outlet count
The documents distinguish the 33-store figure still listed on Aarong's About Us profile from Aarong's official newsroom confirmation that its 34th outlet opened in Dinajpur on 16 February 2026. The files do not assert that this announcement proves the total number of stores currently trading on 25 November 2026; later openings should be checked before publication.

Sources:
- https://www.aarong.com/bgd/about-aarong
- https://www.aarong.com/sg/newspost/aarong-opens-its-34th-outlet-in-dinajpur

### 4. Brand portfolio taxonomy
The project distinguishes the Aarong master brand, four fashion/lifestyle sub-brands (Taaga, Taaga Man, HERSTORY by Aarong and Aarong Earth), and two food-and-beverage concepts (Grassroots Cafe and Terracotta Tales). The latter are not described as fashion sub-brands.

Source: https://www.aarong.com/bgd/about-aarong

### 5. App Data Safety disclosure
Google Play's Data Safety information is described as a developer-provided disclosure. Its statement about encryption is treated as an apparent inconsistency with Aarong's privacy-policy wording, not as an independent test or proof of the app's actual encryption implementation. The recommended next step is an authorized technical review followed by accurate reconciliation of public disclosures.

Sources:
- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.portonics.aarong
- https://www.aarong.com/bgd/privacy-policy-cookie-restriction-mode

### 6. Search Console and public indexing
The audit language now distinguishes public Google search/index checks and rendered-page observations from Google Search Console. No authorized Search Console property access was available to this independent audit; first-party GSC reporting remains conditional on Aarong granting access.

### 7. Traffic estimates
Similarweb and Semrush figures are labelled by platform, month, device/panel and estimate type. The report no longer treats them as interchangeable or calculates a cross-platform traffic multiple. First-party GA4 data are not publicly available.

Sources:
- https://www.similarweb.com/website/aarong.com/
- https://www.semrush.com/trending-websites/bd/apparel-and-fashion

### 8. Instagram commerce recommendations
The strategy now requires checking Bangladesh-specific Meta commerce eligibility before proposing native Shops or product tagging. Trackable bio/Story links and DM-to-site journeys are included as practical alternatives.

Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/facebook-instagram-by-meta/eligibility

### 9. Roadmap feasibility and timing
The US Webstore recommendation has been reframed as a feasibility-gated decision. Demand, customs/tax, payments, fulfilment, returns and support economics should be validated before restoration is promised. Related work was moved out of the first 0–3-month phase.

### 10. Audit-period clarity
The documents distinguish the 90-day content review (23 August–21 November 2026) from website, app-listing and policy snapshot checks performed on 21 November 2026.

## Remaining limitations

1. Aarong's exact live outlet total after the 34th-outlet opening announcement should be checked against a current store locator or official update.
2. Actual app encryption, crash rates and data flows require technical/authorized access; the public Data Safety declaration is not a security test.
3. First-party GA4, Google Search Console, CRM, customer-support, sales and margin data are unavailable to this external audit.
4. Internal financial allocation specific to Aarong is not disclosed in the cited BRAC-wide surplus policy.
5. Native Instagram Shops/product tagging availability must be checked against current Bangladesh-specific Meta eligibility and Aarong's account setup.
6. US Webstore restoration economics require internal demand, customs, payment, fulfilment, returns and service-cost data.
7. Third-party traffic values are modeled estimates, not first-party traffic measurements.

No new primary research, customer survey, app penetration test, or access to private Aarong systems was conducted as part of this quality review.
