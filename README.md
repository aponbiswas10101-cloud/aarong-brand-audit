# Aarong: Brand Audit & Competitor Intelligence

An independent marketing research portfolio project examining Aarong's brand position, selected competitors, publicly observable digital experience, and strategic opportunities in Bangladesh.

> **Project status:** Independent external audit. This work is not commissioned, endorsed, or verified by Aarong or BRAC. It uses publicly available material and third-party estimates; it does not have access to Aarong's internal analytics, financial records, customer databases, or engineering systems.

## Project objectives

- Describe Aarong's brand identity, heritage, BRAC social-enterprise context, artisan ecosystem, and portfolio.
- Benchmark Aarong against Kay Kraft, Deshal, Yellow, and Sailor using comparable public evidence where available.
- Review public website, mobile-app listing, search visibility, content, social-commerce and customer-policy signals.
- Translate the findings into an evidence-based SWOT analysis and a phased 12-month strategic roadmap.

## Evidence and method

Findings are classified as:

- **Verified:** directly supported by an official source or a reproducible public observation.
- **Partially verified:** dependent on public platform limits, samples, or modeled estimates.
- **Analytical interpretation:** reasoned from the observations and comparisons.
- **Needs further research:** requires first-party access, restricted data, or additional primary research.
- **Recommendation:** proposed action, not a claim that Aarong has adopted it.

Website, app-listing and policy observations are snapshots dated 21 November 2026. The YouTube/content review uses a separate 90-day observation window from 23 August to 21 November 2026. Similarweb and Semrush figures are third-party modeled estimates from different periods and panels; they are not treated as like-for-like traffic measurements.

## Core benchmark set

- Aarong (focal brand)
- Kay Kraft
- Deshal
- Yellow
- Sailor

The comparative conclusions are limited to this selected set. They should not be generalized to every Bangladeshi fashion or lifestyle retailer.

## Key corrections recorded in this edition

- The Guinness World Records title is **"Largest craft and artisanal retail store"** (2,636 m², Dhanmondi, achieved 30 October 2025). The in-store Nakshi Kantha installation is a separate feature, not the record category.
- BRAC's published 50/50 surplus model is described as a BRAC-wide enterprise model, not as an audited Aarong-specific allocation or artisan dividend scheme.
- Aarong's About Us profile lists 33 stores, while its newsroom confirms the 34th outlet opened in Dinajpur on 16 February 2026. These two statements are distinguished; the latest live total should be rechecked before publication.
- The brand portfolio is described as the core Aarong brand, four fashion/lifestyle sub-brands, and two food-and-beverage concepts.
- Google Play Data Safety is identified as a developer-provided disclosure, not an independent security test. Actual app encryption requires technical verification.
- Public Google search/index observations are not described as an authorized Google Search Console audit.
- Similarweb and Semrush estimates are not converted into a cross-platform traffic multiple.
- Instagram native Shops/product-tag eligibility is treated as a prerequisite to verify in Bangladesh; trackable links and DM-to-site journeys remain alternatives.
- US Webstore restoration is feasibility-gated rather than promised as an immediate delivery.

## Research limitations

This is an external public-source audit. It cannot verify internal sales, revenue, margins, market share, customer lifetime value, first-party GA4 conversion data, Search Console property data, app crash telemetry, internal privacy/security controls, ad account performance, or private operational plans. Those items are identified as future validation requirements rather than supplied as facts.

## Suggested use

This repository is intended as a marketing research and strategy portfolio sample. Review the methodology, evidence classifications, source log, and limitations alongside the recommendations. Any current operating figure should be rechecked against the source page before use in a live commercial decision.
